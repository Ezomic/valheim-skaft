# Skaft: what the crosshair says about the sweep.
#
# The half that had never been scripted. It needed four things Devkit could not do - damage a
# piece without destroying it, put a build tool in hand with an entry selected, aim the camera
# at something, and read what the crosshair says - so the sweep was tested by hand in one world
# in August and the crosshair line not at all.
#
# Four claims, in order:
#
#   1. Nothing damaged in reach says so.
#   2. Pointing at an INTACT piece with damaged neighbours says to aim at a damaged one. This is
#      the mod's first troubleshooting entry and the thing most likely to be reported as a bug:
#      the sweep only follows a repair vanilla itself just made, so a swing at a whole wall does
#      nothing however much is broken beside it.
#   3. Pointing at a DAMAGED piece counts that piece and its damaged neighbours.
#   4. Below the curve there is no reach, so there is no line at all - not a line reading zero.
#
# It does NOT swing the hammer, and cannot: Player.Repair runs off input, and a scenario has no
# way to press the attack button. So the repairing itself is still the August test and the hand.
# What this covers is everything the crosshair claims about it, which is what a player reads
# before deciding the mod is broken.
#
# THE GEOMETRY MATTERS, so it is written down. Five walls three metres ahead, two metres apart,
# at right -4 -2 0 2 4. From the player the middle one is 3.0m and is therefore what `face` and
# `damage` reach first; its neighbours are 3.6m and the outer pair 5.0m. But the sweep's radius
# is measured from the HOVERED PIECE, not from you - so from the middle wall the neighbours are
# 2m away and the outer pair 4m, and at Crafting 60 all five are within the 8m reach.
#
# It sets your Crafting skill and leaves it at 0, which is where the last claim needs it. That
# is fine on the dev character these are run on; Devkit's Skills pane sets it back if it ever
# is not - and at Crafting 0 this mod writes no crosshair line, which looks like a fault.
#
# Stand on flat ground with about eight metres clear in front of you, and NOT near a real base -
# `wipe` takes down everything built within 14m, not only what this placed.

clear
give Wood:250,Hammer:1
god
ghost
sweep

wipe 14
wait 1
flatten
wait 1

place woodwall 3 -4
place woodwall 3 -2
place woodwall 3 0
place woodwall 3 2
place woodwall 3 4
wait 2

skill Crafting 60

# Repair selected, which is the entry Skaft's ReachEntries names. It is also what puts the game
# in build mode at all, and without build mode there is no hovered piece for any of this to be
# about - UpdateWearNTearHover returns early on !InPlaceMode().
tool Hammer piece_repair
wait 1


# ---- 1. nothing damaged: the line says so rather than saying nothing.

face woodwall
wait 1
hovering woodwall
crosshair Damaged_in_reach:_none


# ---- 2. hovered piece intact, neighbours broken: aim at a damaged one.

# The inner radius is what makes this expressible. Without it the nearest wall is the middle
# one - the one that has to stay whole - and there would be no way to break its neighbours
# without breaking it first.
damage woodwall 60 6 3.3
damage woodwall 60 6 3.3
wait 1

face woodwall
wait 1
hovering woodwall
crosshair aim_at_a_damaged_piece


# ---- 3. hovered piece damaged: it counts itself and the two neighbours.

damage woodwall 60 3.2
wait 1

face woodwall
wait 1
hovering woodwall
crosshair Damaged_in_reach:_3


# ---- 4. no reach, no line.

# Nothing in the world changed - only the skill. At Crafting 0 the radius is zero, and Skaft
# writes no line at all rather than one reading zero: a new character is not told about a
# feature they have not got yet. `nothing` is the sentinel for an empty crosshair, and it is
# not `none` because "Damaged in reach: none" is a real thing this line says.
skill Crafting 0
wait 1
face woodwall
wait 1
crosshair nothing

close
